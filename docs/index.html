Duo Clone

Duo Clone is an app cloner for Android that lets you run multiple accounts of the same app on one phone. Clone an app you already use, sign in with a second account in the copy, and keep your work and personal accounts apart.

Official channel

Google Play is the only official channel for Duo Clone; it is never offered as an APK.

Duo Clone is developed by XLAB (xlabcompany.com). This page is the developer's official page for the app: it carries the release notes and the developer's contact point. The privacy policy is published on the project website.

What Duo Clone does

  • Clones apps you already have, so you can use two accounts or more at the same time.
  • Groups copies by account; apps in the same account share one Google sign-in.
  • Hides copies in a private space behind a fingerprint or PIN lock.
  • Backs up a copy to an encrypted file with a password and restores it later.
  • Shows the memory and storage each copy uses, and lets you force stop a copy or clear its cache.
  • Switches between running copies from a quiet notification or a floating bubble.
  • Lets you change the icon and name of a copy to keep it private on your home screen.
  • Speaks 23 languages, with light and dark themes.

Good to know

  • Apps that ask not to run in a virtual space cannot be cloned. This includes many banking and payment apps.
  • The private space hides copies from other people who use your phone. It does not protect against harmful apps.
  • Some apps may not work fully inside a copy. You can turn on crash reports in Settings to help the developer fix them.
