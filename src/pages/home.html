What Duo Clone does

Clone an app you already use, sign in with a second account in the copy, and keep your work and personal accounts apart.

Good to know before you clone

  • Apps that ask not to run in a virtual space cannot be cloned, including many banking and payment apps. Why apps opt out.
  • Copies keep your accounts apart, but they share one Android user ID: separate logins, not a security sandbox. Where the limits are.
  • Private Space hides copies from other people who use your phone. It does not protect against harmful apps.
  • Some apps may not work fully inside a copy. Turn on crash reports in Settings to help us fix them.
